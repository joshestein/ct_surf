# Surf Forecast Dashboard

One page that shows the surf forecast for the Cape Town Spots I surf.

## Glossary

**Spot**
One surf break with a fixed place. Muizenberg, Big Bay, Long Beach (Kommetjie) and Llandudno are Spots.

**Rating**
surf-forecast.com's score for how good the surf is at one Spot at one time, from 0 to 10. It is the "overall rating".

**Slot**
The forecast for one Spot at one time. It has a Rating, swell and wind. For the next 3 days there is a Slot every 3 hours. For later days there are only morning and afternoon Slots.

**Primary Swell**
The largest swell in a Slot: height, period and direction. Other, smaller swells are not shown.

**Wave Energy**
One number (kJ) that combines swell height and period. It lets me compare swell strength across Spots.

**Wind State**
How the wind meets one Spot: off-shore, cross-offshore, cross-shore, cross-onshore, on-shore, or glassy. The same wind can be off-shore at one Spot and on-shore at another.

**Webcam**
A live video of a Spot, run by someone else. Not every Spot has one.

**Surf Window**
The part of a day when I can surf: from about 1 hour before sunrise until sunset. Only Slots in the Surf Window are shown.
